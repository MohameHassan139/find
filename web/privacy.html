Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026 · Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Find ("Find", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Find mobile application (the "App") on Android and iOS, and any related services (together, the "Service"). Find is a classifieds and listings marketplace app that lets users browse, publish, and respond to ads across categories such as real estate, vehicles, and general goods, and to message other users about those listings.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

Find is operated by Find LLC, registered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. You can reach us using the contact details in Section 11.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

CategoryExamples
Account & authentication Mobile phone number, one-time verification code (OTP), and the authentication session this creates.
Profile information Display name and profile photo, if you choose to add them.
Listings ("ads") you create Title, description, price, category, the city/region you select for the listing, and any photos you upload for that listing.
Messages Text messages you send to other users through the in-app chat about a listing.
Communication preferences Whether you allow other users to contact you via in-app chat, WhatsApp, and/or phone call for a given listing.
Favorites & interactions Listings you mark as favorites, and users you choose to block.
Identity / professional verification (optional) If offered and if you choose to use it: identity verification through Saudi Arabia's national single sign-on service (Nafath), and/or a real-estate brokerage license number (FAL) for agents who want a verified badge. You are never required to complete this to use core features of the App, and this feature is not active in all versions of the App.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, our servers and infrastructure providers automatically process standard technical information necessary to operate the App, such as your IP address, general device and network information, request timestamps, and app version, for the purposes described in Section 3 (primarily security, abuse prevention, and reliability). We do not use this information to build advertising profiles, and the App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2.3 Photos and files

When you add a photo to your profile or a listing, the App opens your device's built-in photo picker. Find only receives the specific image(s) you select — the App does not request, and does not have, general access to your photo library or camera roll.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not access your precise (GPS) location. Location fields in listings and search filters are manually selected by you from a list of cities/regions.
  • We do not access your contacts, call log, or SMS messages.
  • We do not collect payment card details within the App.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify your phone number via OTP.
  • To let you publish, browse, and search listings.
  • To deliver in-app chat messages between buyers and sellers, and to route calls/WhatsApp conversations you choose to initiate.
  • To maintain your favorites, notifications, and blocked-users list.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms (e.g. blocked users, moderated content).
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. How information is shared

  • Other users, by design: your display name, profile photo, listings, and any messages you send are visible to the users you communicate with, as part of how a listings marketplace works. If you enable the "Call" or "WhatsApp" communication channel for a listing, your phone number becomes visible to users who tap that option so they can contact you directly.
  • Service providers: we use third-party infrastructure providers — including cloud hosting and file/image storage (currently Supabase) — to operate the Service. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and confidentiality obligations, and may store data on servers located outside the country where you reside.
  • WhatsApp / phone dialer: if you tap the WhatsApp or Call button on a listing, the App hands off to the WhatsApp app or your device's phone dialer. Any conversation that then takes place happens outside the Find app and is subject to WhatsApp's (Meta's) own privacy policy and your carrier's terms — Find does not receive or store the content of those calls or WhatsApp chats.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Find, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Find is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a policy at least as protective.

5. Data retention

We retain your account information and content for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual listings at any time from "My Ads." You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete Account in the App; this removes your profile and revokes your session. Some information may be retained for a limited period afterward where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our agreements.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit (all traffic between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS; the App does not send data over unencrypted connections). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: you can view and edit your profile information directly in the App.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual listings, or delete your entire account, at any time from within the App (see Section 5).
  • Communication preferences: you can turn the WhatsApp/Call contact options on or off per listing at any time.
  • Blocking: you can block other users so they can no longer contact you.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law — for example, the right to request a copy of your data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. International data transfers

Find is based in Saudi Arabia. If you access the Service from outside this country, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own, including by our infrastructure providers. We take steps intended to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection wherever it is processed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
